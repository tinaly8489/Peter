Rare Animals

Brown Bear

  • Size: Massive omnivore (130 to 400+ kg / 300 to 900+ lbs).
  • Habitat: Widespread across Eurasian and North American taiga.
  • Key Trait: Survives harsh winters through deep hibernation; diet is mostly plants, berries, and nuts, supplemented by scavenging or hunting.

Moose

  • Size: The world's largest deer (up to 700 kg / 1,500 lbs).
  • Habitat: Northern taiga zones, bogs, and forests.
  • Key Trait: Long legs for deep snow, excellent swimming ability, and a strict herbivorous diet of twigs, bark, and aquatic plants.

Tiger

  • Size: One of the largest predators in the taiga.
  • Habitat: Forests of Russia and northern Asia.
  • Key Trait: Excellent hunter with thick fur that helps it survive cold environments.
