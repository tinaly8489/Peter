Rare Animals

Giant Pangolin

  • Armor of Scales: Covered in large, overlapping keratin scales that act as protection against predators like lions and leopards.
  • Specialized Diet: Possesses a long, sticky tongue to feed almost exclusively on ants and termites instead of hunting prey.
  • Burrowing Habit: Spends daytime hours sleeping in deep underground burrows to escape the open savanna heat.

Grévy's Zebra

  • Distinctive Stripes: Features much narrower, closely spaced stripes than other zebra species, and its belly is completely white.
  • Sound Communication: Instead of the typical braying sound, they produce deep, rumbling vocalizations similar to a hippo.
  • Habitat Preference: Prefers the arid scrublands and semi-arid grasslands of East Africa, where they can survive for days without drinking water.

Sonoran Pronghorn

  • Built for Speed: Known as the fastest land mammal in North America, capable of sprinting at speeds up to 60 miles per hour.
  • Desert Adaptation: Suited for arid grasslands and desert scrublands, getting most of its water from the moisture in the plants it eats.
  • Pronged Horns: Both males and females possess horns with a forward-facing prong, which they shed annually.
