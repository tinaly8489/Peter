Rare Animals

Javan Rhino

  • Scientific Name: Rhinoceros sondaicus
  • Habitat: Tropical forest in Java, Indonesia.
  • Diet: Leaves, shoots, and young plants.
  • Status: Critically Endangered.
  • Fun Fact: It is one of the rarest large mammals in the world.

Sumatran Tiger

  • Scientific Name: Panthera tigris sumatrae
  • Habitat: Tropical rainforests of Sumatra, Indonesia.
  • Diet: Deer, wild pigs, and other animals.
  • Status: Critically Endangered.
  • Fun Fact: It is the smallest surviving tiger subspecies.

Northern Muriqui

  • Scientific Name: Brachyteles hypoxanthus
  • Habitat: Atlantic rainforest in Brazil.
  • Diet: Leaves, fruits, and flowers.
  • Status: Critically Endangered.
  • Fun Fact: These monkeys are known for their peaceful social behavior.
